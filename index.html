CloudSync AI: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · App: CloudSync AI (Android, iOS) · Developer: Shadab · Contact: shaikhmohammedshadabs22@gmail.com

Summary. CloudSync AI moves and copies files between storage accounts you connect, such as Google Drive. The app has no servers of its own. Your files and account data go directly between your phone and those providers. We never receive, store or see your files, your passwords or your sign-in tokens.

What the app accesses, and why

DataWhyWhere it stays
Your Google account name, email address and profile pictureTo sign you in (“Continue with Google”) and show which accounts are connectedOn your phone
Files and folders in the accounts you connect: names, sizes, types, dates and contentsTo list your files, show what uses space, and move or copy the files you chooseFile contents pass directly from one provider to the other through your phone’s memory and are not saved on the phone. For larger files between two Google accounts, the app asks Google to make the copy itself: the file is shared privately with the destination account for a moment, without any email, and the share is removed right after. A list of file names and sizes is cached on the phone.

A record of your transfers (which files, sizes, progress, result)To show your transfer history and continue a transfer after the app was closedOn your phone

The app changes files only when you start a transfer. A copy adds files to the destination account. A move adds them to the destination and then puts each original in the source account’s trash, only after its copy has been checked. Trashed files can be restored from the provider’s trash.

Friends’ accounts. You can connect someone else’s account only if they sign in themselves on your phone. They choose the access: either only files the app adds to their account (their own files stay private), or all their files (so their files can be moved or copied). They can remove the app’s access at any time.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect passwords. You sign in on Google’s own page (and later Microsoft’s or Dropbox’s).
  • We don’t run servers that receive your files or data, and the app contains no analytics or advertising code.
  • We don’t sell, rent or share your data, and we don’t use it for advertising.
  • We don’t use data from Google APIs to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CloudSync AI’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Feedback and crash reports

If the app closes unexpectedly, it saves a short technical report on your phone. On the next start it asks whether you want to send it. If you choose Send report (or Settings → Send feedback), your own email app opens with the report so you can read it before sending. Crash reports contain the app version, phone model, Android or iOS version and a technical log with email addresses, tokens and file names removed. Feedback emails contain your primary account's email address, the app version, phone model, Android or iOS version and any recent errors, with other email addresses, tokens and file names removed. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Removing your data

  • Log out in the app removes every connected account, sign-in token, cached file list and transfer record from your phone, and revokes the app’s Google access where possible.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored on the phone.
  • You can revoke access at any time: Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Files you moved or copied stay in the accounts you sent them to. They are your files and are not deleted when you log out.

Children

CloudSync AI is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for consent in your country).

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
